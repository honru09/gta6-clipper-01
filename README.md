The clip captures a high-octane driving sequence of Jason cruising through sunny Vice City in a muscle car before merging onto the highway bridge as an airliner thunders low overhead into downtown Leonida. Rendered into vertical format with cinematic blurred-fill framing and dynamic on-screen text hooks.
